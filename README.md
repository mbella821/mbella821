I am a **Signal Processing Researcher and Research Engineer** working at the intersection of **Signal and image processing, artificial intelligence, inverse problems, and Source Separation**.

My research focuses on a fundamental problem:

> **How can we reliably recover a set of unknown signals from highly noisy and (multi)-source observations?**

I develop **model-based, data-driven, and hybrid signal-processing methods** for scientific applications, including the **ESA Euclid** and **LISA** missions, as well as audio and speech processing.

## 🎙️ Convolutive Blind Source Separation

**Blind Source Separation · Audio · Speech · Time-Frequency Processing**

My research focuses on blind source separation of convolutive and underdetermined mixtures, with applications in audio and speech signal processing.

A particular focus is on separating multiple sources when the number of observations is smaller than the number of sources.

### Developed approaches combining

* 🎵 Time-frequency masking
* 🧩 Relaxed sparsity models
* 📊 Bayesian estimation 
* 📡 Relative Transfer Functions
* 🎯 Beamforming
* 🔀 Time-frequency clustering
* 🔄 Optimal source recombination
